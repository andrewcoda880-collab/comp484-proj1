World Galaxy

Alice Colatrane

1972 album from Alice Coltrane released on Impulse! Records. Sounds very similar to her other spiritual jazz albums that would follow this release, but as the cover suggests this album features a string section that adds a whole other element to the recording that her other releases don't have. This album also features two non-original pieces, My Favorite Things and A Love Supreme, both previously performed by her husband, John Coltrane.
