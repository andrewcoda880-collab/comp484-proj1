Standing On The Corner “II" (2026)

standing on the corner

Third album from the experimental Brooklyn band standing on the corner, released 10 years to the date after their first project. This album feels even more out there and 'sound collage-ey' than their previous stuff, but also somehow sounds more cohesive, despite dabbling in so many different genres on this project - funk, rap, rock, post-rockabilly, and orchestral, to name a few. This album is a great follow up to their previous work, and shows that the band has more gas in the tank, despite not releasing an official project since 2017. I also really love the album cover.

Blame The Clown (2026)

Twisted Teens

New band from New Orleans. Their sound incorporates garage-rock, punk, and blues, but also features a lap steel (I believe a Fender Stringmaster D8) that gives their music a unique vibe. Great raw and abrasive vocals too. This album (and their next one) will hook you in on the first listen.

Happy Today (2025)

Jeff Parker ETA IVet

Really great live jazz album from Jeff Parker and ETA IVet (quartet). Recorded in Highland Park at the Lodge Room, this album highlights some really great guitar, sax, and bass playing, as well as some ridiciously deep drum grooves. The recording quality on this project is also a highlight, especially since it was recorded in a live setting. If you're looking for some jazz music that feels contemporary, but also features a sound palette that doesn't deviate too far from the classic stuff, check this out.

Live at The Opera House at 7A Ranch, Wimberley, TX, Nov 6, 2025

Big Thief

Recently released live album from one of my favorite bands, Big Thief. This album features a bunch of unreleased material, as well as stuff from every album in their discography. The band sounds extremely tight here, I especially love the gutiar playing/tones they achieved here. I hope they release this in a proper album format, for now YouTube will have to do.
